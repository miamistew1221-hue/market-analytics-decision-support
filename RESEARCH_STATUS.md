# Original MES 41-specialist research — status

This repository does not represent a verified implementation of the original frozen 41 detectors. Previous archived research explored a 2R target, $35 modeled maximum trade risk, at most three trades per week, one position at a time, and manual execution. Earlier research ledgers and reconstructed candidate engines were not fully detector-parity certified. Previously reported development-only 1R/2R/3R tests were negative at the portfolio level and are not included as independently verified runnable results here.

To incorporate the original engine, restore the complete frozen source archive and candidate reference data; verify timestamp, side, entry, stop and missing/extra events per detector on causally selected contracts. Do not use already-inspected historical periods as pristine lockboxes. The EMA-cross demo is a software fixture, not a substitute for the frozen system.
